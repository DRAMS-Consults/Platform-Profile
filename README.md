## About DRAMS Consults

DRAMS Consults is building a connected digital healthcare experience designed to make healthcare services more accessible, organized, and convenient.

Our vision is to create a trusted digital front door where patients can discover healthcare services, request consultations, connect with qualified physicians, access health education, and maintain a structured history of their care.

We are bringing consultation management, clinical documentation, health education, and medical merchandise into a coherent digital ecosystem.

**Our guiding principle is simple: make accessing care easier while keeping clinical information secure and continuity of care at the centre of the experience.**

## What We're Building

DRAMS Consults is organized around four core product pipelines.

### 🩺 Telemedicine & Consultations

A digital gateway to professional healthcare services.

Our planned capabilities include:

* Physician and specialist discovery.
* Consultation requests and appointment management.
* Unit-based and specialist consultations.
* Physician availability and scheduling.
* Waitlists and physician assignment.
* Secure video consultation integration.
* Referrals and follow-up workflows.

### 🗂️ Electronic Medical Records

A structured approach to documenting and maintaining patient care histories.

The planned clinical record system will support:

* Patient profiles and relevant medical history.
* Clinical encounters and consultation notes.
* Diagnoses and assessments.
* Investigations and supporting reports.
* Treatment plans and medication information, where applicable.
* Referrals and follow-up records.
* Appropriate access controls and audit trails.

Our objective is to support continuity of care without attempting to replace clinical judgement or the broader capabilities of a hospital information system.

### 📚 Health Education & Digital Content

We believe reliable health information should be easier to discover, understand, and revisit.

The platform will provide a structured content library for:

* Health articles and educational resources.
* Videos and short-form educational content.
* Podcasts and audio resources.
* Ebooks and downloadable publications.
* Categorized health topics and related resources.
* English and Hausa patient-facing content.

### 🩺 Medical Wear & Accessories

A dedicated space for medical wear and selected healthcare accessories.

The planned catalogue will support product discovery, product information, available variants, and a practical purchasing or order-request workflow.

As the platform grows, this pipeline may expand to include more advanced commerce and fulfilment capabilities.

### 🤝 Healthcare Concierge Services

Healthcare often involves more than a single appointment.

DRAMS Consults also envisions a concierge service layer to help coordinate defined healthcare-related requests, referrals, and other approved services.

The goal is to make the next step in a patient's healthcare journey easier to understand and manage.

## The Patient Journey

We are designing the platform around a connected healthcare experience.

**Discover → Learn → Choose a Service → Request a Consultation → Receive Care → Document Care → Follow Up**

Each stage should have a clear purpose, with appropriate handoffs between patient-facing services, clinical workflows, and administrative operations.

The experience is being designed with mobile accessibility and English–Hausa language support in mind.

## Engineering Principles

Our technical decisions are guided by the responsibilities of building a healthcare product.

**Security by Design**

Patient information requires appropriate authentication, authorization, privacy controls, auditability, and secure data handling.

**Clinical Integrity**

The platform should support healthcare professionals and established clinical workflows. It must not be treated as a substitute for professional judgement.

**Modular Architecture**

We aim to maintain clear boundaries between consultations, clinical records, billing, content, merchandise, and other business capabilities.

**Patient-Centred Design**

The platform should make common tasks understandable and accessible, including for patients who primarily use mobile devices.

**Responsible Growth**

We are prioritizing a practical initial release before expanding into more complex integrations, automation, and enterprise capabilities.

**Documentation-Driven Development**

Product requirements, architecture decisions, operational procedures, and development workflows should be documented alongside the software.

## Technology Direction

The initial architecture is being planned around a modern, TypeScript-based web stack.

| Area                 | Proposed technology |
| -------------------- | ------------------- |
| Web application      | Next.js             |
| Programming language | TypeScript          |
| UI styling           | Tailwind CSS        |
| Relational database  | PostgreSQL          |
| Database toolkit     | Drizzle ORM         |
| Content management   | Sanity              |
| Version control      | Git and GitHub      |
| Automation           | GitHub Actions      |

Payment processing, video consultations, authentication, file storage, and notification providers will be selected and integrated according to the project's requirements, security needs, and operational constraints.

*Technology selections and implementation status may evolve as the platform moves through development.*

## Project Status

**Current stage: Product planning and architecture.**

The project is being organized around a focused MVP that prioritizes:

* Patient access and account management.
* Consultation requests and booking workflows.
* Physician and specialist management.
* Foundational electronic medical records.
* Payment and subscription handling.
* Essential health content publishing.
* A lightweight medical merchandise catalogue.
* Core administration and operational workflows.

Advanced enterprise management, deeper commerce capabilities, and additional healthcare integrations are potential future expansions.

Features will be identified as implemented only after they have been developed and appropriately tested.
